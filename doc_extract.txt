PRESENTATION AND ANALYSIS OF THE EXISTING SYSTEM
This chapter presents and analyzes the existing system used by Estandarte Dental Clinic in managing patient appointments, billing, and record keeping. It describes how the current manual process operates, identifies its scope and limitations, and discusses the problems encountered by the clinic. The gathered information serves as the foundation for designing the proposed Dental Clinic Appointment and Billing System.
The Existing System
Estandarte Dental Clinic has been in operation for approximately three years, catering to a growing number of patients in its locality. Despite its continuous service, the clinic still relies on a traditional, paper-based system to manage its daily operations. Appointment scheduling, billing, and record management are all conducted manually, which presents several operational challenges that hinder efficiency and accuracy.
In terms of appointment scheduling, the clinic staff manually record the patient's information and preferred time in a notebook or on paper whenever a patient books an appointment. According to the staff, "if a patient wants to schedule for the next day or morning, we write it down on paper to mark that the slot is booked." This system is straightforward but prone to human error. There are instances when multiple patients request the same time slot, resulting in overlapping appointments. The clinic also encounters difficulties when walk-in patients arrive at the same time as those with prior bookings. The staff explained 
that, in such cases, they follow a first-come, first-served policy, which often causes delays and dissatisfaction among scheduled patients who arrive late. These issues illustrate the limitations of manual documentation in handling dynamic appointment schedules.
When it comes to billing and payment, the process is also done manually. Payments are accepted in cash, with receipts issued by hand. While cash payment is straightforward for patients, the lack of an automated billing system leads to slow processing and occasional inconsistencies. There is no centralized tracking system to monitor payments, outstanding balances, or transaction histories, which increases the possibility of miscalculations or misplaced records. The absence of an automated billing module makes it difficult to generate instant receipts, verify payment history, and ensure accuracy in financial reporting.
Patient records are stored in physical folders that are organized alphabetically and kept in filing cabinets. Each record contains essential information such as personal data, treatment history, and payment details. However, over time, this manual filing system has proven inefficient. The clinic staff admitted that some old records have been misplaced or lost, making it difficult to retrieve information when needed. As the clinic continues to serve more patients, the growing volume of paper files becomes harder to manage, leading to longer retrieval times and reduced data security.
Overall, the existing system of Estandarte Dental Clinic is functional but outdated. It relies heavily on manual effort, which is time-consuming and susceptible to errors. The clinic's growing operations now demand a more reliable, 
efficient, and modernized approach to managing appointments, billing, and patient records.
Scope
The current manual system of the clinic covers only the basic operational functions, including appointment scheduling, billing transactions, and record keeping. It primarily focuses on in-clinic processes, which means that all transactions are conducted face-to-face. Patients who wish to schedule appointments must personally visit or call the clinic. Similarly, payments are made on-site, and patient records are updated only after each visit.
The systemâ€™s scope is limited to handling day-to-day operations without digital integration or automation. Each processâ€”scheduling, billing, and record managementâ€”is handled separately, making it difficult for staff to coordinate information. Then a patient pays for a service, the transaction is recorded in a receipt book, but it is not automatically linked to their appointment or treatment history. This disconnection results in redundant tasks and inconsistent data management.
Furthermore, the existing system does not provide any remote access or online functionality. Patients cannot view, schedule, or cancel appointments online, nor can they access their billing or treatment records. The lack of an automated reminder system also contributes to missed appointments, which affects the clinicâ€™s daily operations and service efficiency.
Survey results from patients reinforce these observations. Many respondents expressed dissatisfaction with the current system, citing that it is inconvenient and time-consuming. The majority agreed that overlapping schedules, lost records, and billing errors are recurring problems. They strongly supported the idea of adopting an automated system that would simplify operations, provide real-time access to records, and improve overall service quality. Based on these findings, it is evident that the existing systemâ€™s scope is too narrow to meet the demands of both the clinic staff and their patients in a digital age.
Concept of Operations
The current concept of operations at Estandarte Dental Clinic revolves around manual workflow and human intervention at every stage of the process. The system starts when a patient books an appointment, either by visiting the clinic or contacting the staff. The detailsâ€”such as name, contact number, and desired date and timeâ€”are written on paper or in a logbook. On the day of the appointment, patients are attended to based on the recorded schedule, but conflicts arise when multiple patients show up at similar times. Walk-ins are accepted but often disrupt the original schedule, forcing the clinic to adjust appointments in real time.
Once the patient's treatment is completed, the billing process begins.
 
The staff calculates the total fee manually, accepts payment in cash, and issues a handwritten receipt. These transactions are then recorded in a separate ledger, which serves as the clinic's financial record.
However, since the process is not automated, there is no immediate wayto verify whether all payments have been properly recorded or matched with patient treatments. This manual billing operation consumes valuable time and increases the likelihood of errors or discrepancies.
The record management process is also paper-based. After each consultation or treatment, the dentist or staff updates the patientâ€™s record by writing the new details on their folder. These records are stored inside a cabinet, and retrieving them during follow-up visits can be tedious, especially when files are misplaced or unorganized. As the clinic accumulates more records over the years, the manual filing system becomes increasingly inefficient.
Survey data from patients further emphasize the shortcomings of this operation. A significant number of respondents indicated that they often experience overlapping schedules and billing confusion, and they find it difficult to reschedule appointments under the current setup. Conversely, most participants strongly agreed that introducing an automated appointment and billing system would make operations faster, more accurate, and more convenient. Many also expressed interest in digital features such as online booking, automated payment receipts, and integration of patient records across services.
Given these insights, the concept of operations clearly shows that the manual system can no longer keep up with the growing demands of the clinic. The proposed Dental Clinic Appointment and Billing System aims to address these operational challenges by integrating scheduling, billing, and record management into a unified digital platform. Through automation, the system will reduce human 
error, improve time management, enhance data accuracy, and provide a more satisfying experience for both the staff and patients.

CHAPTER 5
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE REVIEW
This chapter discusses the actual development and deployment of the Estandarte Dental Clinic Appointment and Billing System. It outlines the steps taken to implement the system, including software installation, configuration, and testing. The chapter also highlights how the system is applied in the clinic's daily operations to ensure efficiency, accuracy, and improved management of appointments and billing processes.
Adopted Architectural
The proposed Estandarte Dental Clinic Appointment and Billing System adopts a serverless three-tier architecture built on the Firebase platform. This architecture separates the system into three main layers: the presentation layer React frontend, the application logic layer Firebase services and Cloud Functions, and the data layer Firestore database and Firebase Storage. This serverless approach eliminates the need for managing physical servers, reduces operational costs, and provides automatic scaling for the clinic's growing needs.
Presentation Layer
The presentation layer serves as the user interface of the system, built using React 18 with TypeScript and Vite as the build tool. It allows users across five distinct roles Admin, Doctor, Staff, and Patient to interact with the system through a web-based environment. The interface uses hash-based SPA routing for navigation and a custom global store for state management. The dark luxury theme with gold and ink palette provides a professional and modern appearance. Users can book appointments, manage patient information, generate bills, view clinical records, and access various dashboards based on their role permissions.
Application Layer (Firebase Services and Cloud Functions)
The application layer contains the core logic of the system, leveraging Firebase services and Cloud Functions. Firebase Authentication handles user login, registration, password reset, and role-based access control with custom claims. Firebase Cloud Functions process server-side operations including email notifications and daily reminders sent at 8 AM for tomorrow's patients. 
When a patient books an appointment, the application layer validates the data, checks available time slots against existing appointments and blocked dates, calculates billing amounts if applicable, communicates with Firestore to store records, and triggers Cloud Functions for notifications. This layer ensures that all system operations follow defined business rules and that data flows correctly between components.
Data Layer
The data layer is responsible for storing all information securely using Cloud Firestore as the NoSQL database and Firebase Storage for file storage. Firestore collections include users all user profiles with role assignments, appointments full appointment lifecycle tracking, services dental service catalog, settings clinic configuration and staff permissions, notifications in-app notifications per user, feedbacks patient star ratings and reviews, logs audit trail, announcements clinic announcements, and waitlist entries for fully-booked dates. Firebase Storage paths store treatment photos.
The three layers work together to provide a smooth workflow: the presentation layer collects user input, the application layer processes and validates it through Firebase services and Cloud Functions, and the data layer stores and retrieves the necessary information from Firestore and Storage. This layered approach promotes efficient operations, ease of maintenance, secure data management, and reliable system performance, making it ideal for a small to medium-scale dental clinic operation.

The system architecture of the proposed Estandarte Dental Clinic Appointment and Billing System is designed to ensure efficiency, reliability, and real-time data processing. Users across five roles access the system through a web browser, where they can perform various actions such as booking appointments, managing clinical records, processing payments, and viewing analytics.

The architecture facilitates real-time synchronization through Firestore listeners, ensuring that every update made by an authorized user is immediately reflected across all connected clients.

10.4 Recommendations
Based on the findings of this study and the feedback received from UTAUT respondents, the following recommendations are proposed for future enhancements and improvements to the Estandarte Dental Clinic Appointment and Billing System.
10.4.1 Technical Recommendations
Mobile Application Development
 - Develop native iOS and Android applications using React Native to provide a better mobile experience for patients and staff, especially for those with limited internet connectivity.
Offline Mode Enhancement
 - Expand the existing Firestore offline persistence to allow full offline functionality for staff, enabling queue management and patient lookup even without internet connection.
Teleconsultation Module
 - Add video call functionality for remote consultations, allowing patients to consult with dentists without visiting the clinic physically.
Electronic Medical Record (EMR) Integration
 - Explore integration with Philippine health information systems for standardized electronic medical records.
10.4.2 Functional Recommendations
Loyalty and Rewards Program
 - Implement a patient loyalty program with points accumulation, discounts for frequent visits, and referral rewards.
Advanced Analytics Dashboard
 - Add predictive analytics for patient no-shows (using historical data), revenue forecasting, and inventory management for dental supplies.
Multi-Branch Support
 - Extend the system to handle multiple clinic locations if Estandarte Dental Clinic expands to additional branches.
Insurance Integration
 - Integrate with health insurance providers for direct billing and claims processing.
Automated Appointment Reminder Customization
 - Allow patients to choose their preferred reminder time (e.g., 24 hours, 12 hours, 2 hours before appointment).
10.4.3 Organizational Recommendations
Regular Staff Training
 - Conduct quarterly refresher training sessions for staff to ensure they are utilizing all system features effectively.
Data Backup Verification
 - Implement monthly verification of Firestore backups to ensure data can be restored if needed.
User Feedback Collection
 - Continue collecting user feedback through the in-app feedback form and conduct annual UTAUT evaluations to measure ongoing user satisfaction.
Security Audit
 - Conduct annual security audits and penetration testing to identify and address potential vulnerabilities.
Documentation Update
 - Maintain and update system documentation including user manuals, API documentation, and deployment guides as the system evolves.
10.5 Conclusion
The Estandarte Dental Clinic Appointment and Billing System has successfully transformed the clinic's operations from a manual, paper-based process to a modern, digital, and automated platform. The system has effectively addressed the identified problems of lack of centralized records, scheduling delays, billing errors, and difficulty generating reports.
The UTAUT evaluation results (Overall WM = 4.52, Excellent) confirm that the system is well-accepted by its users, with strong ratings in Performance Expectancy and Behavioral Intention. The system has improved appointment scheduling efficiency, automated billing processes, enhanced 
patient record management, and provided real-time reporting capabilities for clinic administration.
The successful implementation of this system serves as a model for other small to medium-sized dental clinics in Compostela, Davao de Oro, and the broader Philippines region, demonstrating how modern web technologies (React, Firebase) can be leveraged to improve healthcare service delivery at an affordable cost.
